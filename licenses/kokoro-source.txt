The pinned model card declares Apache-2.0. A copy is bundled as MODEL_CARD.md.
The model renders audible speech. No sample recording is used as a fallback.

Tokenizer vocabulary: kokoro-onnx 0.4.9, config.json (vocab), MIT.
https://pypi.org/project/kokoro-onnx/0.4.9/
https://github.com/thewh1teagle/kokoro-onnx
The vocabulary is preserved in models/kokoro-vocab.json; scripts/fetch-speech-model.py verifies its digest.

Local phonemization: eSpeak-NG 1.52.0 (GPL-3.0-or-later).
https://github.com/espeak-ng/espeak-ng/tree/1.52.0
Source archive SHA-256: bb4338102ff3b49a81423da8a1a158b420124b055b60fa76cfb4b18677130a23
https://codeload.github.com/espeak-ng/espeak-ng/tar.gz/refs/tags/1.52.0
The complete source archive remains reproducibly downloadable. Build instructions
and the two source patches are in scripts/build-phonemizer.py. The executable
statically links eSpeak-NG for IPA phonemes only; audio output is disabled.
Compiled German/English dictionaries, language definitions, and common phoneme
and intonation tables are bundled and checksum-verified. eSpeak does not generate
any of the audible output.
