# Moderated usability rounds

Use two rounds with documented participant selection and consent. Start with
synthetic plans and a disposable calendar. Keep real participant information,
recordings and notes private. Verify permissions, device identity, current build,
locale, accessibility settings and backup policy before each session.

Ask participants to capture a plan, review and correct fields, confirm import,
find an appointment, inspect a proposal, cancel a voice request, and recover from
an unavailable permission or ambiguous target. Calendar writes must follow the
visible button. Observe whether participants understand what will change.

Record task completion, assistance, corrections, time boundaries, misunderstood
labels and accessibility barriers. Distinguish observed actions from moderator
interpretation. Failed, interrupted and declined tasks remain in the denominator.
Do not grade a session from a model's self-assessment.

Use round one to identify problems, then freeze changes and tasks for round two.
Report participant counts, dependencies and selection limits. Repeated sessions
with one participant are not independent evidence. Human listening and real speech
remain distinct from scripted playback checks. Follow the
[collection guide](collection-guide.md) and [consent draft](consent-draft.md).
