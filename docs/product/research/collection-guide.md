# Collecting plan images and German speech

Collect only with explicit, documented purpose and consent. Use opaque participant
and session IDs. Keep originals, consent evidence and linkage private under
`.local`; none belongs in Git, hosted CI or a source ZIP. The
[consent draft](consent-draft.md) must be completed before recruitment.

Capture complete plan pages, readable text and varied real conditions without
exposing unrelated identities. Retain source/page/series relationships. Repeated
photos, crops and augmentations of one plan belong to one dependency group; they
are not independent evaluation examples. Do not infer omitted or illegible fields.

Record representative German requests and natural pauses with the agreed device,
locale and environment. Keep expected intent, actual transcript, response and
human listening assessments distinct. Include cancellation, ambiguity, missing
permissions and confirmation tasks. A successful scripted transcript or audible
sample does not establish real-speech reliability.

Use the private intake/reference tools described in the
[dataset contract](../../development/dataset.md). Human review must separately
approve row/field transcription, coverage, crop geometry, source relationships
and privacy masks. Only approved, lineage-preserving exports may enter training.
Freeze evaluation groups before comparing candidates. Record failed and incomplete
sessions; do not silently replace them with easier cases.

Run [moderated usability sessions](usability-rounds.md) with synthetic examples
first. Store observations and withdrawals privately. Consent is revocable under
the completed study terms; data processing authority cannot be inferred from the
presence of a file or an automated approval marker.
