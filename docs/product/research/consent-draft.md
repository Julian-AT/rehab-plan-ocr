# Research information and consent — incomplete draft

Before use, the study owner must fill in their identity/contact, study purpose,
processing basis, retention period, recipients, withdrawal process and deletion
limits. This template is not an approval or a substitute for that review.

Explain the tasks, expected duration, optional recording and any foreseeable
burden in language the participant understands. Participation is voluntary;
participants may decline tasks or stop. Do not promise anonymity for raw plans or
voice recordings. Explain how opaque IDs and private storage limit access.

Obtain separate choices for plan images, speech recordings, usability observations,
model-development use and any sharing beyond the study team. Record the consent
version, date, reviewer and participant's choices. Blank choices mean no consent.
Do not bundle public publication with private research participation.

Provide a contact and practical withdrawal procedure. Explain which original,
derived and backup copies can be removed and any applicable limits before consent.
Keep consent evidence private and separate from public examples. Use synthetic
material in repository fixtures and demonstrations. See the
[collection guide](collection-guide.md).
